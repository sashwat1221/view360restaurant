A rooftop in Kirtipur, opened in 2026.

Kirtipur sits on a ridge southwest of Kathmandu, and View 360 sits on top of it. From our roof there is nothing in the way. The valley spreads out below, the hills close in around it, and on clear days the Himalayan range shows along the horizon.

You can turn your chair and see all of it. We built the restaurant around that view, and cook the food we want to eat while looking at it: Nepali dishes made the way people here grew up eating them, with plenty of choice for vegetarians and meat eaters alike.
